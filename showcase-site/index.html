True Roster Strength

Where Does Your Team Stand in the League?

Traditional fantasy standings lie. A team with a 9-2 record might just have gotten lucky against low-scoring opponents. Blindside Dynasty ranks your league based on real starting firepower (70%) and future draft capital (30%) to place every team into a clear competitive tier:

  • Dynasty Apex (Young & Dominant): You have top-tier weekly starters and your best players are under 26. Your title window is wide open.
  • Win-Now Goliath (Older & Heavy Hitters): Huge weekly points, but older veterans. Go all-in for the championship this season.
  • Productive Struggle (Loaded Rebuilder): Low immediate points, but stockpiling 1st round draft picks and young breakout studs.
  • Rebuilding Valley (Old & Underperforming): Low points and aging bench depth. Time to liquidate veterans for future draft capital.
Manager Cheat-Sheet: Max PF vs. Record

What is Max PF? It's the total points your team would have scored if you started your optimal lineup every single week.

True Strength = 70% Optimal Weekly Scoring + 30% Future Draft Capital

It eliminates benching regret and head-to-head luck, so you know exactly whether your team is a real championship contender or a lucky pretender.

True Skill vs Schedule Luck

What If You Played Every Team Every Week?

Your actual record is heavily warped by weekly matchups. If you score the 2nd highest points in the league but play the #1 scorer, you get an unfair loss. All-Play measures your true skill:

  • All-Play True Skill: Calculates your record if you played all 9 or 11 opponents every single week.
  • Luck Delta Index: High-contrast status indicators pinpointing Schedule Beneficiaries (lucky wins) vs. High Adversity (unlucky losses).
  • Revenge Game Tracker: Real-time alerts when you play against superstars you previously traded away.
The Luck Delta Formula
Luck Delta % = Actual Win % − All-Play Win %

A positive delta (+15%) indicates a lucky schedule draw, while a negative delta (-12%) proves a team is suffering extreme bad luck despite having a championship-caliber roster.

Per-Snap Impact

Who Truly Gets Open & Produces?

Raw fantasy points can fool you. A player might catch two lucky blown-coverage touchdowns, while another player is dominating every route with zero touchdown luck.

  • Yards Per Route Run (YPRR): The single most reliable measure of wide receiver talent.
  • Rushing Success Rate: Who gets the tough yards and moves the chains on ground plays.
  • Dynasty Longevity Curves: How many prime fantasy years each player has left before value drops.
Why YPRR (Yards Per Route) Matters
YPRR = Total Receiving Yards ÷ Total Pass Routes Run

If a receiver runs 20 routes and gains 60 yards, their YPRR is 3.00. Any score over 2.50 YPRR is elite WR1 territory (think Justin Jefferson or Malik Nabers).
