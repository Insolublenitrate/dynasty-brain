Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

Waiver Wiretap does not collect or store sensitive passwords. When using the platform, we ingest publicly available league metadata from Sleeper APIs (such as public usernames, rosters, transactions, and league standings).

2. How We Use Information

We use league data strictly to generate quantitative rankings, trade autopsy assessments, and Coach Madden AI gameplans for your personal viewing and league standings.

3. Email Subscriptions

If you subscribe to updates, your email address is used solely to notify you of feature releases and product updates. We do not sell or share your contact info with third parties.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to cache your selected league preferences and active theme settings for an instantaneous user experience.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at bigdillengineering@gmail.com.
